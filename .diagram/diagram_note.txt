FSCM DIAGRAM NOTES

- Authoritative editable sources are the Mermaid `.mmd` files in this directory.
- Render with a Mermaid tool that supports C4Context, C4Container, and C4Component.
- Keep diagrams aligned with `.docs/business/README.md` and `.docs/architecture/repository-skeleton.md`.
- PostgreSQL is authoritative; mobile SQLite is cache and an offline command queue.
- Retailers do not self-order. Drivers do not have accounts or an application.
- Route planning/optimization and regional-to-regional rebalancing are not approved coding scope.
- The Draw.io XML/PNG system-context files are legacy pre-baseline exports; use
  `system-context-diagram/context_diagram.mmd` for current reports.
